Cluck Old Hen is a well-known Appalachian tune thought to date from
the mid-1800s.\footnote{\url{http://folkslingers.com/cluck-old-hen/}
  Folkslingers} This tune most likely has African American roots along
the same vein as the Uncle Remus/Br'er Rabbit stories with similar
rhymes/lyrics found in \textbf{Negro Folk Rhymes; Wise and Otherwise}
by Thomas W. Talley (New York, 1922).\footnote{For a pdf of the book:
  \url{https://baz.link/nfr} For similar rhymes see Little Red Hen
  (p. 37) and The Old Hen Cackled (p. 50).} Cluck Old Hen may have
started as a banjo tune but has found a welcome home among fiddlers
often adding left-hand pizzicatos in the B part imitating a hen
clucking (this can be heard in the Fiddlin' Cowan Powers \& Family
recording).
